\section{Stakeholder Analysis}
\label{section:stakeholder-analysis}

Stakeholders are individuals or groups who either operate the system or are directly affected by its outputs. HelmTrack has three primary stakeholders and one category of affected parties.

\subsection{Traffic Police Officers}
\begin{subsecbody}
Traffic police officers are the primary users of HelmTrack. They are responsible for reviewing violation records generated by the system and taking enforcement action. HelmTrack removes the need for continuous manual CCTV monitoring by delivering automatically generated, evidence-backed reports. Officers need the system to produce accurate and clearly documented violations so that fines can be issued with confidence.
\end{subsecbody}

\subsection{City and Municipal Authorities}
\begin{subsecbody}
City and municipal authorities oversee road safety policy and the allocation of enforcement resources. They require access to aggregated violation data by location and time period so they can identify high-risk areas, measure the effectiveness of enforcement campaigns, and make data-driven decisions about where to deploy officers or install additional cameras.
\end{subsecbody}

\subsection{System Administrators}
\begin{subsecbody}
System administrators are the technical staff responsible for deploying, configuring, and maintaining HelmTrack. They need the system to be stable, configurable with existing CCTV infrastructure, and easy to monitor for performance issues or hardware failures.
\end{subsecbody}

\subsection{Affected Parties: Road Users}
\begin{subsecbody}
Although motorcycle riders and road users do not interact with HelmTrack directly, they are directly affected by its outputs. A false positive — where the system incorrectly flags a helmeted rider as a violator — would result in an unjust fine issued to an innocent person. This makes detection accuracy a critical concern not only for operational effectiveness but also for fairness. Road users have a reasonable expectation that any automated enforcement system produces reliable and verifiable evidence before a penalty is applied.
\end{subsecbody}

\section{User Stories and Use Cases}
\label{section:user-stories-use-cases}

\subsection{Traffic Police Officers}
\begin{subsecbody}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=80pt, labelwidth=60pt, itemsep=4pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item[\textbf{US-P-01}] As a traffic police officer, I want to view a list of detected helmet violations so that I can review evidence and issue fines without manually watching CCTV footage.
    \item[\textbf{US-P-02}] As a traffic police officer, I want to see the license plate number and captured image for each violation so that I can identify the offending vehicle.
    \item[\textbf{US-P-03}] As a traffic police officer, I want to filter violations by date and location so that I can manage records more efficiently.
    \item[\textbf{US-P-04}] As a traffic police officer, I want each violation record to include a confidence score so that I can prioritize high-confidence cases and manually review borderline ones.
\end{description}
\end{subsecbody}

\subsection{City and Municipal Authorities}
\begin{subsecbody}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=80pt, labelwidth=60pt, itemsep=4pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item[\textbf{US-C-01}] As a city authority, I want to view violation statistics by location and time so that I can identify high-risk areas and allocate enforcement resources effectively.
    \item[\textbf{US-C-02}] As a city authority, I want to export violation reports so that I can use the data to support road safety policy decisions.
\end{description}
\end{subsecbody}

\subsection{System Administrators}
\begin{subsecbody}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=80pt, labelwidth=60pt, itemsep=4pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item[\textbf{US-A-01}] As a system administrator, I want to connect HelmTrack to existing CCTV streams so that no new camera hardware is required.
    \item[\textbf{US-A-02}] As a system administrator, I want to monitor system health and receive alerts on failures so that HelmTrack operates reliably over time.
\end{description}
\end{subsecbody}

\subsection{UC-01: Detect and Log a Helmet Violation}
\begin{subsecbody}
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|p{3.5cm}|X|}
\hline
\textbf{Use Case ID} & UC-01 \\
\hline
\textbf{Use Case Name} & Detect and Log a Helmet Violation \\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & System (Automated) \\
\hline
\textbf{Trigger} & A video frame from a CCTV stream is processed by HelmTrack \\
\hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The system is running and connected to at least one CCTV stream \\
\hline
\textbf{Main Flow} &
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=20pt, topsep=0pt, itemsep=0pt]
    \item The system receives a video frame from the CCTV stream
    \item YOLOv8 detects motorcycles, riders, helmets, and license plates in the frame
    \item The system uses spatial association to link each rider to the nearest motorcycle and license plate
    \item The system checks whether a helmet is detected on each rider
    \item If no helmet is detected, the system flags the frame as a violation
    \item The system applies OCR on the associated license plate region to extract the plate number
    \item The violation record is saved with the frame image, plate image, plate number, timestamp, camera location, and confidence score
\end{enumerate} \\
\hline
\textbf{Alternative Flow} & If no license plate is detected, the violation is still logged with the frame image and timestamp, with the plate number marked as unreadable \\
\hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & A structured violation record is stored in the system and available for officer review \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{subsecbody}

\subsection{UC-02: Review Violation Records}
\begin{subsecbody}
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|p{3.5cm}|X|}
\hline
\textbf{Use Case ID} & UC-02 \\
\hline
\textbf{Use Case Name} & Review Violation Records \\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Traffic Police Officer \\
\hline
\textbf{Trigger} & The officer opens the violation review dashboard \\
\hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The officer is logged in and at least one violation record exists in the system \\
\hline
\textbf{Main Flow} &
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=20pt, topsep=0pt, itemsep=0pt]
    \item The officer opens the violation dashboard
    \item The system displays a list of violation records sorted by date
    \item The officer optionally filters records by date range or camera location
    \item The officer selects a violation record
    \item The system displays the full record including the frame image, plate image, plate number, timestamp, and confidence score
    \item The officer reviews the evidence and decides whether to issue a fine
\end{enumerate} \\
\hline
\textbf{Alternative Flow} & If the confidence score is below a threshold, the record is marked for manual review and highlighted in the dashboard \\
\hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & The officer has reviewed the violation and taken appropriate enforcement action \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{subsecbody}

\subsection{UC-03: Export Violation Report}
\begin{subsecbody}
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|p{3.5cm}|X|}
\hline
\textbf{Use Case ID} & UC-03 \\
\hline
\textbf{Use Case Name} & Export Violation Report \\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & City and Municipal Authority \\
\hline
\textbf{Trigger} & The authority requests a violation summary report \\
\hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The authority is logged in and violation records exist within the selected date range \\
\hline
\textbf{Main Flow} &
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=20pt, topsep=0pt, itemsep=0pt]
    \item The authority opens the reporting section of the dashboard
    \item The authority selects a date range and camera location
    \item The system aggregates violation data for the selected filters
    \item The system generates a report summarizing total violations, violation rate by location, and time distribution
    \item The authority exports the report
\end{enumerate} \\
\hline
\textbf{Alternative Flow} & If no violations exist for the selected filters, the system displays a message indicating no data is available \\
\hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & The authority receives a structured report for use in road safety planning \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{subsecbody}
